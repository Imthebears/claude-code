## Hlavní verze (EN)

Hi, I'm Nathan, and welcome to Human Lore.

I make documentary videos about the topics that fascinate me: forgotten history, strange true stories, lost civilizations, unsolved mysteries and the people behind them. No clickbait, no fluff, just well-researched stories told the way I'd want to hear them.

If you love falling down rabbit holes as much as I do, subscribe and join me for the next story.
